Pearl's Eye Photography

A modern, minimal, and visually engaging website designed for a professional photographer to highlight their work and turn visitors into clients.

ROLE UI/UX Designer
CATEGORY UI/UX Design
TIMELINE 7–10 Days
TOOLS Figma, Unsplash, Google Fonts
00 — HERO PREVIEW Click to inspect high-resolution screen
PROJECT OVERVIEW

Photographers often lack a centralized, professionally designed space to showcase their work and attract high-value clients. The goal was to build a responsive, modern website with clear CTAs and focused layouts to elevate the photographer's brand and improve client engagement.

Design Summary
