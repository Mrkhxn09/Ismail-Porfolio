04 — SOCIAL MEDIA & GRAPHIC SELF-INITIATED

Pulse Healthcare Campaign

Educational social media design system, multi-slide carousel frameworks, and visual assets designed to communicate health topics clearly.

ROLE Social Media Designer
TIMELINE 2 Weeks
DISCIPLINE Social Media & Graphic Design
TOOLS Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop
01 // BUSINESS & CONTENT GOAL

Making Health Information Clear and Approachable

Complex health topics are frequently presented in dry, clinical formats or sensationalized clickbait. Pulse was conceived to demonstrate how structured social media design and clear visual hierarchy can make wellness education engaging, credible, and easy to digest.

02 // CONTENT DIRECTION & HOOK STRATEGY

Structured Multi-Slide Carousel Architecture

Established a consistent 4-part carousel structure to guide readers smoothly from discovery to takeaway:

  • Slide 1 (The Hook): High-contrast typography with a clear question or topic statement.
  • Slides 2–5 (The Core Framework): Bite-sized insights, clean vector diagrams, and simplified explanations.
  • Slide 6 (Actionable Summary): Key takeaways formatted for effortless screenshotting and bookmarking.
  • Slide 7 (Discussion / Share): Friendly call-to-action inviting audience questions and saves.
03 // VISUAL SYSTEM & TYPOGRAPHY

Calming Palettes & Editorial Clarity

Developed a cohesive visual identity featuring clean sans-serif typography, calming restorative greens, and warm accent tones.

Restorative Teal #2FA79B
Warm Amber Alert #E8862E
Deep Slate Base #0F1216
Paper White #F4EFEB
04 // CAROUSEL DESIGN & INFOGRAPHICS

Modular Multi-Slide Template Suite

Constructed reusable Figma auto-layout templates allowing rapid creation of educational carousels with consistent margins, typographic scale, and icon styling.

05 // SOCIAL ASSETS & DELIVERABLES

Multi-Format Social Asset Suite

Produced an integrated suite of feed posts, story templates, and promotional banner formats for Instagram and LinkedIn.

Modular 10-Slide Carousel Template Kit (1:1 and 4:5 ratios)
High-Contrast Story & Reel Cover Layouts (9:16 ratio)
Custom Vector Health & Wellness Iconography
Figma Social Media Asset Library with Component Variants
06 // FINAL CONTENT SYSTEM

Design Summary

Pulse Healthcare demonstrates how clean graphic design principles and thoughtful layout hierarchy can elevate social media content into authoritative, shareable visual communication.
