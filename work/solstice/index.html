03 — BRAND IDENTITY & PACKAGING CONCEPT PROJECT

Solstice Specialty Coffee

Brand identity system, bespoke packaging architecture, and retail collateral for an artisanal single-origin coffee roastery.

ROLE Brand Designer
TIMELINE 3 Weeks
DISCIPLINE Brand Identity & Packaging
TOOLS Illustrator, Photoshop
01 // BRAND BRIEF

Artisanal Craftsmanship Meets Modern Editorial Style

Solstice is a conceptual specialty roastery focused on transparent single-origin sourcing and precision small-batch roasting. The project required a distinctive visual identity that bridges Scandinavian minimalist restraint with the tactile warmth of coffee culture.

02 // BRAND DIRECTION & POSITIONING

The Warmth of the Solar Solstice

The creative direction drew inspiration from the celestial solstice—the solar cycle that nurtures high-altitude coffee plants. This was translated into circular solar geometry, rich warm amber hues, earthy kraft tones, and refined editorial typography.

03 // LOGO & WORDMARK DESIGN

Custom Typography Wordmark & Solar Emblem

Designed a custom serif wordmark with balanced letterforms and distinct ligatures, paired with a circular solar seal suitable for wax stamps, cup sleeves, and packaging badges.

  • Custom Wordmark: Refined serif letterforms with distinctive balance and high legibility at all scales.
  • The Solar Emblem: A circular secondary mark used across merchandise, bag seals, and digital avatars.
  • Grid Alignment: Precise geometric proportions for versatile application across horizontal and stacked lockups.
04 // TYPOGRAPHY & COLOR SYSTEM

Warm Editorial Palette & Typographic Hierarchy

Constructed a cohesive color system that pairs deep volcanic charcoal with radiant amber accents and unbleached cream tones.

Solstice Amber #E8862E
Espresso Black #161412
Unbleached Cream #D9CEBA
Forest Roast Green #263328
05 // PACKAGING ARCHITECTURE

Modular Coffee Bag Label Grid

Designed a modular packaging system that allows rotating seasonal single-origin releases without changing the base bag stock. A standardized matte pouch is customized through color-coded origin labels detailing country, altitude, process, and tasting notes.

06 // RETAIL & BRAND APPLICATIONS

Cafe Collateral & Brand Touchpoints

Extended the visual identity across takeaway cups, retail menus, merchandise, packaging tape, and digital social templates.

Primary Wordmark & Secondary Monogram Assets (SVG / EPS)
Modular Coffee Packaging Label Templates
Cafe Menu & Takeaway Cup Collateral
Brand Identity Guidelines Book
07 // FINAL BRAND

Brand Identity Summary

Solstice Specialty Coffee showcases a complete end-to-end identity system that feels elevated, timeless, and thoughtfully crafted across both digital and physical touchpoints.
