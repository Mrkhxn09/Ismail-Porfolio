01 // DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

The Craft of Reduction and Geometric Precision

A logomark is the purest distillation of visual identity. It must communicate character, memorability, and balance whether displayed at 16x16 pixels as a browser favicon or scaled to large architectural signage. The Aether collection explores the intersection of mathematical grids, negative space, and semantic meaning.

02 // GRID SYSTEMS & GEOMETRY

Constructive Optical Grids

Every symbol in the collection is constructed from fundamental geometric primitives—concentric circles, isometric diagonals, and balanced line weights—tested for optical balance rather than purely algorithmic snapping.

  • Monoline Consistency: Unified stroke weights calibrated to remain crisp across all display densities.
  • Negative Space Harmony: Intentional counter-spaces that allow the marks to breathe on both dark and light canvases.
  • Contrast Verification: Every mark is tested in pure monochrome before color exploration.
  • Silhouette Recognition: Instantly identifiable outlines when blurred or viewed from a distance.
