05 — MARKETING & AD CREATIVE CONCEPT PROJECT

Veloce Performance Ad Kit

Promotional marketing assets and high-impact social ad creatives designed for modern digital campaigns and product drops.

ROLE Marketing Designer
TIMELINE 2 Weeks
DISCIPLINE Ad Creative & Social
TOOLS Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma
01 // CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVE

Creating High-Impact Promotional Visuals

Designed a performance-oriented digital advertising kit for an athletic apparel product launch. The goal was to develop striking ad layouts that capture attention in fast-scrolling feeds while maintaining a clean, premium brand aesthetic.

02 // VISUAL HIERARCHY & HOOK FORMULA

3-Tier Ad Structure for Instant Comprehension

Each ad variation was built upon a clear 3-tier visual hierarchy to deliver the core message within 2 seconds of viewing:

  • Visual Anchor: Dynamic product imagery positioned with strong diagonal motion lines.
  • Bold Value Proposition: Concise, punchy headline in high-contrast monospaced badge styling.
  • Frictionless CTA: Prominent call-to-action button with clear next-step direction.
03 // COLOR & TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

High-Energy Contrast Palette

Utilized an energetic amber and charcoal palette with bold athletic typography to communicate speed, technical engineering, and durability.

Velocity Amber #E8862E
Asphalt Black #0B0D0F
Pure White Text #FFFFFF
Teal Accent Tag #2FA79B
04 // MULTI-RATIO ASSET MATRIX

Comprehensive Asset Deliverables

Created coordinated ad sets across all standard social and display aspect ratios for seamless omnichannel deployment.

1:1 Square Feed Ads for Instagram and Facebook
9:16 Full-Screen Vertical Creatives for Stories and TikTok
16:9 Landscape Promo Banners for Web and Desktop
Responsive Component Templates in Figma
05 // FINAL CONTENT SYSTEM

Design Summary

The Veloce Ad Kit highlights how strategic graphic design, strong typographic structure, and dynamic product framing can drive engagement in digital marketing campaigns.
