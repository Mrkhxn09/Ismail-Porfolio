★ Featured Project

Fintra —
Banking, simplified.

Role Lead UI/UX Designer
Timeline 12 Weeks
fintra.app/dashboard
16:9

Problem

Users were dropping out of onboarding at a 40% rate, and the existing dashboard buried the one number people actually opened the app to check.

Solution

Rebuilt onboarding into three lightweight steps and redesigned the dashboard around a single glanceable spend summary, backed by a shared component library.

Results

Onboarding completion rose to 89%, navigation-related support tickets dropped by half, and the component library now powers three other internal tools.

Clip 03 — Process

Five steps,
one straight line.

01

Research

Understanding the user, the business goal and the constraints before a single pixel gets placed.

02

Wireframe

Low-fidelity structure — laying out flow and hierarchy before committing to any visual direction.

03

UI Design

Typography, color and detail applied on top of the structure — where the product starts to feel real.

04

Prototype

Interactive, clickable, testable — so decisions get made on how it feels, not just how it looks.

05

Handoff

Clean specs, organized files and documentation — built so a dev team can ship it without guesswork.
