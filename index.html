Clip 01 — About

Still frames
and moving ones.

I'm Ismail — a graphic designer, UI/UX designer and video editor who treats every project like it has a timeline, whether it moves or not. A poster has a rhythm. A screen has a cut. A scroll has a pace.

I work across brand identity, product interfaces and edited film, moving the same eye for composition, contrast and timing between the still frame and the moving one. Based on the belief that good craft is felt before it's noticed.
